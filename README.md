# Find the song in an Instagram Reel, TikTok, or X video

A short clip plays a track and never names it. Sometimes the app already has the answer. Sometimes you need the audio fingerprinted against a music catalog. This guide covers both paths, using only steps that match how [Reel Song Finder](https://reelsongfinder.com/find-song-from-video) works today.

## Try the free route on the app first

Skip this when you only have a saved file and no post to reopen.

- **TikTok** — Tap the sound name under the video, or the spinning disc. A real title and artist means you are done. Search it in Spotify or Apple Music. If the page says `original sound - @name`, TikTok has no label; that username is the uploader, not the artist. See the longer walkthrough for a [TikTok song finder](https://reelsongfinder.com/guides/tiktok-song-finder) flow.
- **Instagram Reel** — Open the Reel full screen, Share → Copy link. Progress bars across the top mean you are on a Story, not a Reel. Captions and hashtags are not song credits.
- **X (Twitter)** — There is no music sticker and no sound page. Read the post, replies, and quote posts before you paste anything.
- **YouTube** — Expand the description for "Music in this video" or a creator credit. Check chapters and the pinned comment. YouTube links cannot be pasted into the tool (details below).

## Paste a public Reel, TikTok, or X link

When the app never names the track, open [find the song from a video](https://reelsongfinder.com/find-song-from-video).

1. Copy the share link from Instagram (`instagram.com/reel/...`), TikTok, or X (`x.com` / `twitter.com`).
2. Paste it into the box labeled **Video link** (placeholder: "Paste a Reel, TikTok or X link…").
3. Click **Find song**. Status shows "Listening to Reel audio…" on every accepted platform. Volume can stay off; the audio is pulled from the link.
4. A locked card shows a masked title/artist. Unlock when you want the name. After unlock you get title and artist, a ~30-second preview when the catalog provides one, and buttons to open the track on Spotify and Apple Music. Share result, Copy link, and Get ISRC are also there.

Public links work better than holding a phone mic up to speakers. The fingerprint uses the clean audio from the post, not a room recording that mixes speech, noise, and compression.

### YouTube links are rejected

`youtube.com/watch`, `youtu.be`, and `youtube.com/shorts` are not accepted. The banner is exact:

> We can’t open links from there yet. Upload a screen recording and we’ll find the song.

Use the screen-recording path below (or YouTube's own description credit first).

## Screen-recording fallback

Use this when you have no shareable link (saved video, private post, YouTube, or any clip where the remote fetch fails).

1. Record **device / system audio**, not mic-only. Mic-only files fail.
   - iPhone: Control Center screen recorder, microphone **off**
   - Android: built-in recorder with device sound
   - Desktop: record system audio
2. Start where the song starts. About 20 seconds is enough. Large uploads are reduced in the browser to the first 30 seconds of audio.
3. On the site, click **No link? Upload a screen recording instead**, then **Choose audio / video**.
4. Accepted types: MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG.

## When there is no match

"No match" means this audio is not in the catalog. The tool does not invent a title. Common reasons:

- Original / unreleased tracks that never hit major catalogs
- Speech-only or heavy voiceover over the music
- Private accounts or private posts that cannot be fetched
- Humming (not supported)

**No match or failed extract is never charged.** You pay only when you unlock a result.

## ISRC basics

After unlock, **Get ISRC** returns the recording code when the catalog has one. ISRC (International Standard Recording Code) identifies a specific recording, not just a song title. That matters when two versions share a name (radio edit vs. extended mix, cover vs. original). For a dedicated lookup flow, use the [ISRC lookup](https://reelsongfinder.com/isrc-lookup) page.

## What this does not do

- Read captions, on-screen text, or hashtags as the song name
- Accept YouTube paste (upload a clip instead)
- Identify humming or whistling
- Fetch private Instagram / TikTok / X posts
